Method

    Each EU member state is represented by its national CSIRT under the NIS2 Directive. Where a country has separate government or sector teams, they appear under Related teams in the profile, and Spain's CCN-CERT is scored separately because it offers its own public services. EU-level bodies (CERT-EU, ENISA) and free community services (Shadowserver, abuse.ch, DIVD, MISP) are scored on the same rubric for reference. For international comparison, 18 national teams outside the EU are included: the UK, Switzerland, Norway, Ukraine, the US (CISA and CERT/CC), Canada, Brazil, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, India, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Australia, New Zealand and Israel.

    Scores reflect public information found through web research in October 2026, including official pages, RFC 2350 profiles, annual reports and reputable press. Services a team offers only to its regulated constituency score as limited. These are editorial assessments for orientation, not audits. Check each team's site before relying on a detail, and report corrections through the repository.

    The feed panel is filled by a scheduled GitHub Action that tries each team's known feed URLs, then auto-discovers feeds on the team's homepage. Teams without a working public feed are listed as such.
