## Sound effects

- Combat, creature, horn and ambient sounds: The Battle for Wesnoth project (data/core/sounds), GNU GPL v2+.
  Authors include Lari Nieminen (zookeeper), Richard Kettering (Jetrel), Scott Klempner, J.W. Bjerk (Eleazar) and chessmaty.
  Files were trimmed, peak-normalized and converted to MP3.
- Interface clicks (`ui_tap`, `ui_tap2`, `ui_open`, `ui_close`): Kenney UI Audio pack, CC0 — https://kenney.nl (mirror: https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-ui-audio).

A per-file mapping to the original source paths is in `audio/credits.json`.
